Privacy Policy

ReviseSnap helps students create and revise notes. This policy explains what we (don’t) collect.

Data we process

  • On-device processing: Photo OCR, PDF text extraction, audio demo ASR and notes are processed in your browser. By default, data stays on your device (LocalStorage) unless you export it.
  • No account required: The app works without sign-up. We don’t store personal data on servers in this version.
  • Optional cloud features: If enabled later, files will be sent to the chosen provider only for processing and not retained longer than necessary.

Permissions

Camera & Microphone are used locally; you can deny them anytime.

Analytics

No third-party analytics in this version.
